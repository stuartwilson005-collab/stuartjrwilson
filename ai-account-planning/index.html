How I used Custify and AI to cut down the manual work of keeping strategic account plans up to date, while making sure CSMs stayed in control of the final result.

The context

This is an example of how I used AI alongside Custify at Solidatus to cut down the manual work involved in keeping strategic account plans up to date. I also used AI to help put this page together.

01 / The challenge

Keeping a detailed account plan current should not mean rebuilding it every month.

At Solidatus, strategic account plans began as substantial Excel workbooks covering stakeholders, use cases, risks, SWOT, account notes and customer health. They were useful but required recurring manual effort to review changes across multiple sections.

As correspondence and account context accumulated, the administrative work of reconstructing what had changed competed with time better spent on customer decisions and engagement.

02 / The approach

Get the account information organised. Then use AI to help keep it current.

I configured Custify to bring our account plans out of spreadsheets and into a set of views covering areas such as stakeholders, use cases, risks and customer health. Each view showed the latest note with the relevant tag, so the plan could stay current as new notes were added. Customer communications and other account information were already being captured in Custify.

Structured context

Custify views showed the latest tagged note for each part of the account plan.

Regular AI review

On an approximately monthly cycle, AI reviewed account information and added notes with the relevant tags.

CSM ownership

CSMs checked the changes, corrected anything inaccurate and remained accountable for the account plan.

03 / The workflow

AI added the notes. The CSM checked they were right.

AI reviewed the account information and added appropriately tagged notes. Custify then displayed the latest matching note in each account-plan view. The CSM checked the information and corrected anything that needed attention.

04 / What changed

Less time pulling information together. More time acting on it.

Instead of working through every section of a detailed plan and writing updates manually, the CSM reviewed the latest tagged notes. These could reflect changes in usage, approaching renewals, new champions or developing risks.

Before: manual reconstruction
  • Review account correspondence and usage context
  • Work through multiple planning sections
  • Identify changes and refresh notes individually
After: AI-assisted updates
  • AI reviews recent account information
  • AI adds appropriately tagged notes that appear in the account-plan views
  • CSM reviews and corrects the notes before acting on them
The CSM remained accountable.Custify showed the latest note for each tag rather than holding changes in a separate approval area. The CSM remained responsible for checking the information, editing a note or adding a new one if needed and deciding what action to take.
05 / The outcome

Less admin. More time with customers.

AI took on much of the recurring work of reviewing account information and creating updated notes. CSMs still checked accuracy and made the final judgement, but spent less time on account maintenance and had more time to speak to customers, understand their needs and help them get value from the product.
