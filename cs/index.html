About me

Commercially minded. Customer focused. Comfortable with complexity.

My career has centred on understanding what customers want to achieve, helping them get there and building relationships that last.

I've worked across B2B SaaS, developer platforms and enterprise software, with everyone from technical teams to senior executives. That experience has taught me how to connect what a product does with what matters to the customer.

I bring both hands-on experience managing important customer relationships and experience improving how Customer Success teams operate. That means spotting risks early, improving adoption, supporting retention and growth, coaching colleagues and putting practical processes in place.

I enjoy solving customer problems, working closely with other teams and finding better ways to deliver results. Whether I'm working directly with customers or helping a team improve, my focus is always on making a meaningful difference to customer outcomes and business performance.
